Why MBrutals?

Modern, playful, unapologetically bold.

Everything is designed to feel like one coherent design language instead of a pile of generic Bootstrap-looking components.

⚡

Micro Motion

Cards lift, buttons press, inputs react and panels arrive with lightweight motion.

✦

Two Font Modes

Switch between Archivo Black + Space Grotesk or Syne + Poppins.

▣

Real Forms

Inputs, selects, textarea, checkbox, radio, toggle and range controls have their own visual system.

☰

Sidebars

Left, right and top panels are built into the vanilla JavaScript layer.

◆

Flexible Cards

Use the same card primitives as a 2-column grid or a clean list layout.

↗

CDN Friendly

Ship dist/mbrutals.min.css and dist/mbrutals.min.js directly from your own CDN.
