WHY MBRUTALS

Not Bootstrap.
A visual language.

MBrutals is designed around tactile surfaces and expressive composition. Components are built to feel like objects: they lift, press, wobble, slide, and react.

01✦

Every interaction has weight.

Buttons physically press. Cards lift. Inputs respond. Panels slide into view. Motion is part of the component, not an afterthought.

02◈

Custom controls

Forms are drawn from scratch instead of relying on browser-default styling.

03⌁

Responsive by design

Grid, cards, navigation, sidebars and forms adapt cleanly.

04↗

Framework agnostic

Use it in HTML, PHP, Laravel, Astro, static sites, or anywhere CSS works.

05✓

CDN ready

Publish dist and you're ready to ship.
